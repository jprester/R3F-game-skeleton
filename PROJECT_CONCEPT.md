Project concept: realistic tactical magic FPS

The long-term idea is an action FPS set in a modern or near-future high-tech/cyberpunk world, where the protagonist comes from an advanced magical civilization and operates as an elite covert/special-operations agent.

The core premise is not “modern soldier with magic” and not “medieval fantasy character confused by technology.” Instead, the protagonist comes from a civilization where magic has been optimized, engineered, standardized, and militarized to a very high level. Their society is not technologically backward; it followed a different technological tree based on magic.

The protagonist is highly competent, rational, professional, and trained for extra-world operations. They do not cling to fantasy aesthetics or rituals. No chanting, long spellcasting animations, theatrical gestures, or primitive magical practices. Magic is fast, silent, precise, and designed for professional use.

The central fantasy is that magic gives the protagonist access to capabilities modern military systems fundamentally do not have: teleportation, spatial manipulation, perception editing, metaphysical tracking, autonomous magical constructs, subtle defensive fields, nonlethal incapacitation, and manipulation of identity, attention, or topology.

The protagonist should feel like an advanced magical special operator rather than a traditional wizard.

Core worldbuilding principles

Magic is mature technology.
Spells and artifacts have undergone centuries of refinement. Many difficult effects are artifact-assisted, just as advanced modern capabilities depend on industrial infrastructure.
Magic is strongest where modern technology is weakest.
Modern weapons are already very good at raw destruction. Magic is more interesting when it controls:
perception
information
identity
space
causality
attention
mobility
tracking
access
battlefield geometry
Magic should usually be subtle.
Professional magic is low-signature. The more optimized a spell is, the less visually dramatic it tends to be.
Heavy magic is escalation.
If the protagonist is using spectacular large-scale effects, something has gone wrong operationally.
Security forces should be competent.
The protagonist should overpower them because her capabilities violate their assumptions, not because they are stupid.
The protagonist prefers:
avoid contact if possible;
incapacitate if necessary;
use lethal force only when the threat cannot be safely neutralized.
The protagonist uses the minimum intervention needed to solve a problem.
One guard should not require a topology puzzle if a simple Motor Lock or Sleep Pulse will do.

General narrative direction

The preferred story is relatively focused, not initially about a huge geopolitical conflict between worlds.

The protagonist is sent from the magical world into a modern/cyberpunk Earth-like city for a specific covert mission.

Suggested mission:
A major high-tech corporation has acquired a dangerous artifact from the protagonist’s world, possibly a Transit Core capable of creating stable cross-world passages.

The protagonist’s initial orders:

infiltrate the facility
recover the artifact
determine how it arrived
erase evidence where practical
extract

The corporation is not necessarily evil. It found something impossible and is studying it.

During the mission, the protagonist discovers the artifact has already been partially activated and may be connected to something neither side fully understands.

This opens a larger mystery:

someone else may have crossed worlds first
another magical faction may be involved
an unknown extra-dimensional intelligence may be present
the mission expands beyond a simple recovery

Visual tone:
grounded military thriller + cyberpunk + advanced engineered magic.

The magical operative should visually contrast the city:
the city is bright, networked, technological, surveilled;
her magic is dark, quiet, precise, subtle, and alien.

Protagonist

The protagonist is an elite female special-operations / intelligence operative from an advanced magical civilization.

Visual design:

dark messy hair
calm, focused, professional demeanor
black tactical-magical armor
asymmetric dark wayfarer cloak/mantle
long black combat staff
subtle magical artifacts attached to belt/harness
small hovering recon constructs
guardian shards
black crystalline seeker munitions
dark graphite / steel / restrained blue-white visual palette
no conventional wizard robes

Her competence should feel procedural and disciplined:
she scouts, verifies, chooses the safest solution, incapacitates threats efficiently, and moves on.

She should not constantly perform flashy acrobatics or needlessly complex solutions.

Spatial Anchors
Small devices that establish safe teleport destinations.

Important safety concept:
teleportation uses a verified arrival volume, not a coordinate point.

Safety sequence:
probe → authenticate → reserve destination volume → translate → verify → release origin

If the destination is blocked, tampered with, moved, or unsafe:
teleportation simply fails.

No normal “telefragging.”

Teleportation safety features:

protected arrival volume
environmental continuity check
anchor authentication
dynamic obstruction sensing
fail-closed behavior
fallback destinations
remote inspection
tamper alarms
freshness verification
return-to-origin or rollback capability

Core spells

Blink
Short-range teleport.
Should be:

fast
reliable
destination-verified
subtle

Visual:
brief spatial smear, duplicated silhouette, frame-skip effect, or local distortion.

Veil
Suppresses attention rather than creating literal invisibility.

Observers may see the operative but interpret her as unimportant or fail to focus on her.

Useful for:

crossing occupied spaces
avoiding escalation
social infiltration

Silence
Creates a small region where sound transmission is suppressed or redirected.

Motor Lock
Temporarily disables voluntary movement while preserving breathing and circulation.

One of the main nonlethal tools.

Sleep Pulse
Induces rapid controlled unconsciousness.

Should be safe and precisely regulated.

Sensory Blank
Temporarily suppresses hearing and/or vision.

Equilibrium Break
Disrupts balance and spatial orientation.

Weapon Bind
Temporarily disables a weapon or mechanism.

Concept:
moving parts behave as though locked into one solid object.

Disarm Translation
Teleports a carried weapon or dangerous object a short distance away.

Barrier
Short-lived directional defensive field.

Deflection
Redirects incoming trajectories rather than fully stopping them.

Restraint
Applies magical bindings to an incapacitated target.

Remote Touch
Limited remote physical interaction.

Mark
Tags a person or object for:

tracking
seeker guidance
follow-up effects

Trace
Reads recent interactions or magical residue.

Echo
Reconstructs fragments of recent events.

Life Sense
Detects living beings through nearby obstacles.

Fold
Creates temporary adjacency between spaces.

Translate
Longer-range teleportation through a verified anchor.

Perception Edit
Makes observers misclassify what they saw.

Memory Blur
Degrades a short recent memory.

Illusory Decoy
Creates a limited false target.

Probability Nudge
Biases plausible uncertain outcomes.
Should be expensive and restrained.

Visual language for magic

Core principle:
Most spells should manifest as distortions in reality, not bright energy beams.

Direct-control spells
Visual:

brief shimmer
tiny geometric flicker
pressure ripple
faint black-silver line

Motor Lock
A narrow distortion connects staff to target.
For a split second, a faint geometric body-map appears.
Then motor control stops.

Concussive force
Should look like a transparent shock front.
Rain, dust, paper, and loose fabric reveal the effect.

Spatial magic
Visual:

refracted geometry
duplicated edges
impossible parallax
thin crystalline seams
wrong perspective
starless depth
temporary non-Euclidean surfaces

Barrier
Not a blue bubble.
Instead:

bent reflections
doubled edges
rain deflecting around an invisible plane
incoming bullets changing trajectory
faint polygonal facets

Blink
No explosion.
Possible visuals:

multiple offset silhouettes
brief frame-skip
spatial smear
momentary discontinuity

Fold
A region shows the wrong perspective.
A wall may briefly reveal a corridor that should not be behind it.

Veil
Barely visible.
The protagonist becomes perceptually unimportant rather than transparent.

Weapon Bind
Dark lines briefly trace mechanical joints.
The weapon becomes physically locked.

Seeker Crystals
Minimal visual trail.
They are unsettling because of intelligent movement.

Guardian Shards
Idle orbit → sudden defensive snap into position → brief transparent micro-shield.

Sleep Pulse
Very subtle ring in humid air, faint nervous-system flicker, target collapses.

Life Sense
Mostly visible only to the protagonist through magical perception.

Heavy magic
Breaks the visual restraint:

obvious spatial deformation
curved rain
impossible reflections
multiplied constructs
strong environmental distortion

Initial environment:

Initial player abilities:

Action philosophy

The protagonist should usually choose the simplest and safest solution.

Examples:

Unaware guard:
avoid them.

Alert guard:
Motor Lock or Sleep Pulse.

Squad:
area suppression or targeted nonlethal effects before they acquire the operative.

Locked door:
Fold or bypass.

Camera:
Veil, perception manipulation, or make it irrelevant.

Incoming fire:
Guardian Shards / Deflection / Barrier.

Dangerous opponent that cannot be safely incapacitated:
escalate.

The protagonist should not do elaborate tricks when a direct solution exists.

Useful one-sentence project pitch

Magic wins the situation; force resolves only what remains.

Avoid if possible. Incapacitate if necessary. Escalate only when required.
